# AYN

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users and purpose

Job seekers browse company-sourced jobs, maintain their profile and resume, compare job requirements, and create tailored documents. Employers request reviewed access to search the opted-in talent pool, assess candidates, and send proposals.

## Capabilities and constraints

Existing React/Vite and Supabase application. The public checker stays text-only. Paid generation, optimization and tailoring retain their existing billing, authentication and factual safeguards. There is no autofill or automated application submission. Preserve real routes and existing tools. Do not invent hiring guarantees, testimonials, job counts or payment outcomes.

## Brand commitments

The founder approved preserving AYN's real logo and charcoal/ember colors, simpler navigation, compact job results, a document-focused resume workspace, quieter buttons/cards and mobile-specific layouts. Existing assets and product truth take priority over design conventions.

## Evidence

Product and integration truth: docs/map/. Tests use explicitly synthetic fixtures, not fabricated production results. Existing paid flow reliability work is tracked separately in docs/release-readiness.md.
