# AYN Service Level Agreement

Version: 1.0
Effective: 1 August 2026

This Agreement applies to Employers on a paid plan. It does not apply to the free trial month, to job seeker plans, or to features labelled beta, preview, or experimental.

---

## 1. Uptime commitment

AYN will use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Platform available at least **99.0%** of the time in each calendar month, measured as set out below.

99.0% allows for approximately 7 hours 18 minutes of unavailability in a 30 day month. We have set this deliberately rather than claiming a number we cannot stand behind. AYN depends on third party infrastructure whose own commitments sit at or near this level, so promising more would be promising something outside our control.

## 2. How availability is measured

**Available** means the Platform responds to a valid request without a server error.

**Unavailable** means requests to core functionality fail with a server side error for a sustained period of thirty consecutive minutes or more, as determined from AYN's own records. AYN's records are the sole basis for calculating availability.

Monthly uptime is calculated as total minutes in the month, minus unavailable minutes, minus excluded minutes, divided by total minutes in the month, minus excluded minutes.

Core functionality means signing in, candidate search, sending proposals, sending and taking assessments, and resume and cover letter generation.

## 3. What is excluded

The following do not count as unavailability:

- **Scheduled maintenance**, announced at least 48 hours in advance and limited to 4 hours in any calendar month, performed outside 09:00 to 18:00 Atlantic Time on business days wherever practical.
- **Emergency maintenance** required to address a security vulnerability or prevent imminent data loss, announced as soon as we reasonably can.
- **Third party failure** outside our control, including outages at our database, hosting, AI, or payment providers, and general internet or DNS failures.
- **Your own act or omission**, including misconfiguration, exceeding plan limits, or suspension for non payment or breach.
- **Force majeure.**
- **Suspension or restriction** of your account under the Terms of Service.
- **Degraded AI output quality**, which is a matter of accuracy rather than availability and is addressed in section 4 of the Terms of Service.

## 4. Service credits

If monthly uptime falls below the commitment, you may claim a credit against your next invoice:

| Monthly uptime | Credit |
|---|---|
| 97.0% to below 99.0% | 5% of that month's fee |
| 95.0% to below 97.0% | 10% of that month's fee |
| Below 95.0% | 25% of that month's fee |

Credits are available on the Growth and Scale plans only.

**Claiming.** Email support@aynn.io within fifteen days of the end of the affected month, with the dates, times, and supporting evidence of the unavailability you experienced. Claims made after that period, or without evidence, are not eligible. We will respond within a reasonable time.

**Limits.** Credits in any calendar month will not exceed 25% of that month's fee, and credits in any twelve month period will not exceed the fees paid in one month. Credits are applied to future invoices, are not paid in cash, do not extend your term, and expire on termination.

**Sole remedy.** Service credits are your only remedy for failure to meet the uptime commitment. This does not limit your right to terminate under section 5.

## 5. Chronic failure

If monthly uptime falls below 95.0% in three consecutive months, you may terminate your subscription on written notice given within fifteen days of the third such month. Termination is your sole remedy under this section and no refund is payable.

Business days are Monday to Friday excluding Nova Scotia statutory holidays. These are aims for a first response, not resolution, and are not commitments. Failure to meet them does not entitle you to a credit or any other remedy.

## 7. Status and incident communication

We will use reasonable efforts to notify affected Employers by email of any prolonged incident. We do not commit to a specific notification time, to publishing a status page, or to providing a post incident report.

## 8. Changes

We may amend this Agreement on thirty days' notice, effective from your next renewal. We will not reduce the uptime commitment during a term you have already paid for.
