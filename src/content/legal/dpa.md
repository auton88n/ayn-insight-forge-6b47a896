## 1. Why this Agreement has two halves

Most data processing agreements assume one party instructs and the other obeys. That is not an accurate description of AYN, and pretending otherwise would leave both of us exposed. AYN plays two different roles depending on whose data is involved.

**AYN is an independent controller** of job seeker personal data in the talent pool. Job seekers give their information to AYN, consent to AYN making them discoverable, and can withdraw from AYN at any time. AYN decides how that pool is maintained, how profiles are represented for matching, and what a searching Employer is permitted to see. You do not instruct AYN in any of this, and AYN does not act on your behalf when doing it.

**AYN is your processor** for the data that belongs to your account: your company profile, the roles you describe, your search history, your proposals, and the assessment records generated for you. AYN processes that on your instructions and for no other purpose.

**You become an independent controller** of a candidate's personal data at the moment it is disclosed to you, which is when that candidate accepts your proposal. From that point AYN has no control over your use of it and you are solely responsible for it.

Section 3 governs the first and third of these. Sections 4 to 12 govern the second.

## 2. Definitions

"Data Protection Law" means the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR, the Swiss FADP, PIPEDA, Quebec Law 25, the CCPA and CPRA, and any other applicable law governing the processing of personal data.

"Candidate Data" means personal data relating to a job seeker.

"Employer Data" means personal data within your account, including your users' account details, your company profile, role specifications, searches, proposals, and assessment records.

"Sub-processor" means a third party engaged by AYN to process Employer Data.

Controller, processor, personal data, processing, data subject, and personal data breach carry the meanings given in the UK and EU GDPR.

## 3. Controller to controller terms for Candidate Data

**3.1** In respect of Candidate Data, AYN and you are each independent controllers. Neither is the other's processor, and this Agreement does not create joint controllership.

**3.2 AYN's undertakings.** AYN will:
- Obtain each job seeker's explicit, recorded, and withdrawable consent before making them discoverable;
- Withhold each candidate's name, email address, telephone number, and postal address from you until that candidate accepts your proposal, enforced at the server layer rather than in the interface;
- Label inferred attributes as inferred wherever they appear, and prevent an inferred attribute from satisfying a requirement you have marked mandatory;
- Remove a candidate from the pool immediately on withdrawal of consent;
- Handle data subject requests directed to AYN in respect of the pool.

**3.3 Your undertakings.** You will:
- Use Candidate Data solely to evaluate that candidate for the role for which they were identified;
- Not export Candidate Data into any external system, or disclose it to any third party, without that candidate's separate consent;
- Not attempt to identify or contact a candidate outside the Platform before they accept your proposal;
- Determine your own lawful basis for your processing, and provide any notice your own law requires;
- Handle data subject requests directed to you, and respond to them within the periods your law requires;
- Notify AYN without undue delay if you become aware of a breach affecting Candidate Data in your possession.

**3.4 Automated processing.** AYN provides automated fit assessment, candidate ordering, and assessment evaluation. **AYN is the developer of those systems; you are their deployer and the decision maker.** Any obligation your law places on the deployer of an automated employment decision tool, including bias auditing, candidate notice, disclosure, provision of an alternative process, and record keeping, is yours. AYN will provide information reasonably available to it to assist you, and doing so does not transfer any obligation to AYN.

## 4. Processor terms: scope and instructions

**4.1** For Employer Data, AYN acts only on your documented instructions. Your instructions consist of the Terms of Service, this Agreement, and your use of the Platform's features.

**4.2** AYN will notify you if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes Data Protection Law. AYN may suspend performance of that instruction until it is amended or confirmed.

**4.3** AYN will not sell Employer Data, share it for cross context behavioural advertising, or retain, use, or disclose it for any purpose other than performing the Terms of Service.

**4.4 Subject matter and duration.** Processing continues for the term of your subscription and the retention periods in the Privacy Policy.

**4.5 Nature and purpose.** Hosting, storage, generation and evaluation of text, search, billing, support, and security.

**4.6 Categories of data subject.** Your personnel with account access, and job seekers whose records appear in your searches, proposals, and assessments.

**4.7 Types of personal data.** Names, business contact details, company information, role descriptions, search parameters, proposal content, and assessment questions, responses, timings, and evaluations.

**4.8 Special category data.** AYN does not require special category data and asks you not to enter it. If your role descriptions or messages contain it, you are responsible for the lawful basis.

## 5. Confidentiality

AYN limits access to Employer Data to personnel who need it to deliver or support the service, and binds them to confidentiality obligations that survive their engagement.

## 6. Security

AYN maintains technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk. As at the date of this Agreement those measures include:
- Encryption in transit and at rest;
- Row level database access controls restricting each account to its own records;
- Scoped, revocable tokens for the browser extension rather than stored credentials;
- Administrative access gated by a role check and a second factor;
- Audit logging of privileged actions;
- Service role isolation for assessment rubrics and results, which are not readable by any application role.

This list describes AYN's measures as at the date of this Agreement. It is not a warranty that any specific control will remain in place. AYN may change these measures, including replacing a control with a different one, provided the overall level of protection is not materially reduced. No security measure is guaranteed to prevent every incident.

## 8. International transfers

AYN stores Employer Data in the United Kingdom. Generation and evaluation of text, payment processing, and analytics involve transfers to the United States.

For transfers from the United Kingdom, the parties incorporate the UK International Data Transfer Addendum. For transfers from the EEA, the parties incorporate the European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses, module one where both parties act as controllers under section 3, and module two where AYN acts as processor under sections 4 to 12. Where the Clauses require an option to be selected: docking clause applies, the audit and sub-processor provisions in this Agreement apply, governing law is Ireland, and the supervisory authority is that of the Member State in which you are established.

In the event of conflict, the Clauses prevail over this Agreement.

## 11. Breach notification

AYN will notify you without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Employer Data, and will provide the information required by Article 33(3) of the UK and EU GDPR so far as it is available to AYN at the time.

AYN is not required to provide information it does not hold, to investigate beyond its own systems, or to notify your data subjects or any supervisory authority on your behalf. Those are your responsibilities as controller. Notification is not an admission of fault or liability.

## 12. Audit

AYN will make available information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this Agreement, and will respond to a reasonable written security questionnaire not more than once in any twelve month period.

Where a supervisory authority formally requires an audit that this information cannot satisfy, AYN will permit one on at least sixty days' written notice, during business hours, once only in any twenty four month period, by an independent auditor agreed in advance and not a competitor of AYN, subject to confidentiality undertakings, limited in scope to the systems processing your Employer Data, conducted so as not to disrupt the service or affect any other customer, and entirely at your cost including AYN's reasonable time.

AYN retains what it is legally required to retain, including transaction records, and retains aggregated and de-identified data that does not identify you or any individual.
