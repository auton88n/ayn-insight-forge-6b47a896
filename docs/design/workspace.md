# Workspace redesign

Mode: Operate for search, checker and account tools; Read for help/legal; Persuade for existing product/pricing explanations.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Help a job seeker move from a real posting to a prepared document without competing screens of decorative cards.

OWN-WORLD: Preserve the AYN wordmark, charcoal text and ember action color. Neutral canvas, white working surfaces, restrained separators, 8px controls and 12px panels. No decorative motion or glass layers.

STORY: Search and read first, inspect the fit, then prepare a document from the existing profile. Prices and unresolved issues remain visible. All existing secondary tools stay reachable.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 224px navigation rail and broad work area. Search controls sit above a compact results list and reading pane. Mobile shows results or the chosen job, not a description buried after 25 results. Profile presents Resume, Profile facts and Preferences as clear views, with readable document preview.

FORM: The founder explicitly approved this direction on 25 September. The optional concept engine could not run (not installed); no generated direction or comp is claimed. Implementation follows the approved existing brand and structure directly.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

## Verification

Desktop/mobile search, selection and return, stale-request handling, checker continuation, navigation/Back, profile previews and restore affordances. No production account or payment mutations during visual testing. Record limits for authenticated journeys not tested with a real account.
